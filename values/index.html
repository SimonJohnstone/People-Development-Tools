1 Below are 55 values. For the first part of this exercise, select which values feel important to you. Discard any that aren't important by leaving them in the un-filed pile. Don't worry about limiting the number of values.
  • Which ones did you discard? Why?
  • Were there any that you weren't sure of?
2 Now you need to select your top 20. Drag values from your Important Values pile into the Top 20. If you're struggling with choosing, try to put yourself in a situation where you compromised that value. How did it feel?
  • How was that? Did you struggle with any, or did you find it was easy picking out your top 20?
3 Now it gets even harder. Select your top 10 from your top 20. Can't decide? Ask your teammates if they think you demonstrate that value.
4 Finally, select your top 3 values.
  • What made you choose these three?
  • Are there any that almost made the cut?
  • Did you ever compromise any of these values?

Moving cards: tap + on a card to move it up to the next column, or − to move it back. You can also drag them with the mouse. On a phone, press and hold a card, then drop it on the next column, or on a tab at the bottom to send it to that column. Drop it on another card to reorder. With a keyboard, press Tab to reach a card, then Alt + ↑/↓ to reorder, and Alt + ←/→ to move between columns.
