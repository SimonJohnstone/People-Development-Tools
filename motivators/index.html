How to use this board

⌄

Think about one of your best days at work — the day you left thinking "Wow, that was a great day!" You were probably doing something motivating, energising, and engaging.

  1. Browse the Motivators list and tap + on the ones that resonate to add them to My 10.
  2. In My 10, tap + on your most essential motivators to move them to Top 5. Tap − to move a card back.
  3. Open Settings, enter your email, and tap Export PDF to save a landscape summary.
